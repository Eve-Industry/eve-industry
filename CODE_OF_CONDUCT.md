# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, doxxing and spam are not acceptable in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who break these rules.
Report problems privately through the repository's "Security" tab contact
route or by contacting a maintainer directly.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies
as the full standard.
